About

"A wise man once said"

Leandroyarni started with one simple belief: a leather jacket shouldn't be something you replace every year. It should be something you live in. Every jacket we pick is chosen for how it feels the first day you wear it and how it looks years later, after it has softened, creased, and shaped itself to you.

We keep our collection small on purpose. Instead of chasing trends, we focus on timeless cuts like the biker, the bomber, and the racer. We care about strong stitching, quality zips, and details that hold up to real life.

When you buy from Leandroyarni, you're not just buying a jacket. You're getting a piece that can become part of your story.

Wear it loud. Wear it for years.
